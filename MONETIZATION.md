# Monetization Strategy

To generate revenue from the Nowruz countdown site without compromising UX, consider the following unintrusive methods:

## 1. Native Sponsorship / Brand Integration (Recommended)
Partner with relevant businesses (e.g., travel agencies, gift shops, Persian restaurants) to sponsor the countdown.
- **Example**: "This countdown is brought to you by [Brand Name]" with a subtle logo in the footer or near the countdown.
- **Placement**: Non-intrusive banner below the countdown or in the footer.

## 2. Affiliate Marketing (Contextual)
Promote products related to Nowruz traditions.
- **Haft-Sin Items**: Beautiful Haft-Sin sets (Amazon/Digikala affiliates).
- **Books/Poetry**: Links to Hafez or Shahnameh books.
- **Flowers/Gifts**: Affiliate links to flower delivery services for New Year gifts.
- **Placement**: Under the "Traditions" section as "Recommended resources".

## 3. Premium Features (Freemium)
Offer basic site for free, but add premium features for logged-in users.
- **Example**: Personalized greeting cards generator (e.g., "Send a custom Nowruz card to friends").
- **Example**: SMS reminders for the exact moment of delivery.

## 4. Subtle Display Ads (AdSense/Carbon)
If you must use display ads, use high-quality networks like Carbon Ads which are designed for developers/designers and look cleaner.
- **Avoid**: Pop-ups, sticky videos, or interstitial ads.
- **Placement**: A single, clean ad unit in the footer or sidebar (on desktop).

## 5. Donations / Support
Add a "Buy me a coffee" link for users who appreciate the clean, ad-free experience.
- **Platform**: Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, or direct crypto options.
- **Context**: "Keep this site ad-free by supporting the developer."
